Privacy Policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

Read the Terms of Service

The short version: this website doesn’t ask for your personal information, and we never sell anything about you. Here’s exactly what gets collected and why.

What this website collects

Nothing you type in. There are no forms, accounts, or sign-ups on this site.

Like most websites, the server that hosts it keeps basic technical logs, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you viewed, and when. We only use these to keep the site running and secure. We don’t use analytics or advertising trackers.

Google Maps

The map on our Visit section is provided by Google, and the “Get Directions” buttons open Google Maps. When the map loads, Google may collect information and set cookies as described in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Cookies

This site doesn’t set any cookies of its own. The only cookies you may see come from the embedded Google map.

When you call or bring in your vehicle

At the shop, we keep only what we need to do the job right:

  • Your name and phone number, so we can reach you about your vehicle.
  • Your vehicle’s year, make, model, VIN, and mileage.
  • A record of the work done, so we have your service history next time.

New York State requires inspection stations to keep inspection records, so those are kept for as long as the state says.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell or rent your information. We only share it when it’s needed to do the work or when the law requires it, for example:

  • Reporting inspection results to the New York State DMV.
  • Giving a parts supplier your vehicle details to order the right part.

Children

This site isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can call us any time to ask what records we have for you, or to have something corrected.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
